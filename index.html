About Becca

Faith-led communication with warmth and creativity

Becca's journey began with a deep conviction that words and gestures can build bridges. With an inspiring mix of faith, artistry and education, she supports learners, ministries and communicators who want to express their message with dignity, clarity, and grace.

Her story is rooted in service: helping people discover American Sign Language, empowering creators through meaningful storytelling, teaching violin with soulful precision, and crafting content that reflects belief, beauty, and inclusion.

Across her work, Becca brings a calm teaching presence and a careful ear for people. She pays attention to the learner, the message, and the moment, so every class, story, or creative support feels personal instead of generic.

Abba's Joy is the home for that blend of faith, communication, creativity, and inclusion: a place where learning can feel practical, thoughtful, and warmly human.

What guides Abba's Joy

Every part of Abba's Joy is shaped by faith-inspired content that uplifts and informs, clear communication for diverse and inclusive communities, creative education that honors each learner's path, and professional support for ministries, businesses, and families.

FAQs & Class Policy

Helpful answers before you book

Who can learn ASL with Becca?

Anyone interested in learning ASL can book a session, whether for personal growth, ministry, family, work, or community engagement.

Are classes beginner-friendly?

Yes. Beginners start with fingerspelling, greetings, everyday vocabulary, simple sentence structure, and Deaf culture.

How are lessons delivered?

Sessions can be held physically or virtually through Zoom or Google Meet, depending on your location and preference.

Where are physical sessions available?

Physical sessions are available in Lagos and Ibadan. Online lessons are available for learners outside those locations.

How much is a beginner session?

Beginner ASL sessions are ₦12,500 per session, with each session lasting one hour.

How often should I take classes?

Two sessions per week are recommended for steady progress, but pacing can be adjusted to fit your goals.

When is payment made?

Payment is made in advance. Your payment confirms your booking and shows commitment to the selected class schedule.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes. Rescheduling requires at least 24 hours’ notice so the session time can be properly adjusted.

What happens if I miss a class?

Missed sessions without prior notice are forfeited. Learners are encouraged to be punctual and consistent.

Are fees refundable?

Fees are non-refundable once sessions commence. Active participation and practice are essential for progress.
